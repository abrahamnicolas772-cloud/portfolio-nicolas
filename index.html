Who's behind NicoLabs?

One simple mission: turning ideas into impactful products.

I'm Nicolas Abraham Nicolson, a Full-Stack Developer, UI/UX Designer, and Prompt Engineer passionate about creating impactful digital solutions. I combine the power of code, design precision, and artificial intelligence to bring unique experiences to life.

With 6 years of experience, I support startups, nonprofits, and businesses in bringing their web, mobile, and AI projects to life — from showcase websites to complex SaaS applications.

My philosophy: code is a tool, design is an emotion, AI is an evolution. Every project is designed to deliver a user experience that's smooth, memorable, and innovative.

Founder of NicoLabs.dev, I put my expertise at the service of those who want to bring their ideas to life on the web, using modern, sustainable, and intelligent technologies.

My approach is simple: understand your needs, propose tailored solutions, and deliver a product that exceeds your expectations. Your success is my priority.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know.

01 What are your delivery timelines?

I work in 2-week sprints. A showcase website is delivered in 2-3 weeks, a full web application in 4-8 weeks depending on complexity. Each project includes a revision phase to ensure your satisfaction.

02 What types of projects do you take on?

I handle showcase websites, web applications (SaaS, dashboards), e-commerce, custom platforms, website redesigns, and AI integrations. If you're unsure, reach out and let's discuss!

03 How does the collaboration process work?

1) Initial discussion to understand your needs — 2) Detailed proposal with quote and timeline — 3) Design & validation — 4) Sprint-based development — 5) Testing & approval — 6) Launch and follow-up.

04 Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes, I offer monthly maintenance plans including: security updates, backups, bug fixes, minor enhancements, and responsive technical support within 24 hours.

05 Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely! I work with clients worldwide. Communication can be in French or English, via video call, email, or instant messaging. I adapt to your timezone.
